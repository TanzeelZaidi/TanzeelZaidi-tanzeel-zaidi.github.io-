Who I Am

Background

I am a Computer Science Lecturer and researcher based in Punjab, Pakistan. With a Master's degree in Computer Science (CGPA 3.67), I specialize in Blockchain Security, Cybersecurity, IoT Access Control, and Artificial Intelligence. I currently lecture at The University of Faisalabad and am pursuing a PhD in AI and Blockchain Security.

Research Focus

My research sits at the intersection of Blockchain, RBAC/ABAC access control, IoT security, and Large Language Models. I have published in IEEE and am actively exploring how LLMs can automate smart contract generation and vulnerability detection in blockchain-based systems.

Teaching Philosophy

I believe in connecting theory to real-world application. Over 3+ years across universities and colleges, I have taught HCI, Computer Graphics, MIS, Cybersecurity, and ICT — always bridging the gap between concepts and industry.

Currently Exploring
